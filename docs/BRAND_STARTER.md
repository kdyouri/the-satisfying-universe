## Visual direction

Deep midnight blue, pearl white and soft iridescent cyan/lilac. A simple luminous
orb with concentric ripples should remain legible in a small circular avatar.
Use consistent icon geometry across platforms. Banner: quiet macro textures,
centered brand name/tagline, generous safe margins. Final raster assets and
platform-specific dimensions are a later verified M2 deliverable.

Do not overlay the profile branding on the videos. Use platform AI disclosure
settings where required; keep descriptions honest about the production process.
